مثال: Cities will probably stop growing. Energy may become too expensive.

Real Conditionals — جمل الشرط الحقيقي

If + present → will / might / should + infinitive

  • If you visit the park, you might not find them all!
  • If you visit during the working week, you should avoid the crowds.
  • If you are interested in perfume, you will love the frankincense trees.

▼
✦ Grammar — would (مواقف خيالية)

'd / would + infinitive — نتكلم عن شيء غير حقيقي أو خيالي

  • My ideal home would be like the place I live now

  • I'd like to have a big kitchen
  • It would have really big windows

✦ Vocabulary

Describing Homes

  • Outdoor: barbecue, pool, garden, balcony, parking
  • Indoor: fireplace, air conditioning, wooden floors, living room
  • Describing: comfortable, spacious, modern, traditional, large, cool, warm
  • Location: close to / views of / faces north / just around the corner

Pros and Cons (إيجابيات وسلبيات)

  • The best/good thing about Dubai is, it's so cosmopolitan
  • The worst/bad thing about Dubai is, there's too much traffic
  • The trouble is, it's quite expensive
  • The thing is, Dubai is too popular

Solving Problems
